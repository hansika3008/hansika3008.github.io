PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

LifeCost is a browser-based tool that helps users estimate the long-term cost of purchases, compare products and understand subscription expenses. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information You Enter

Information entered into LifeCost's calculators, such as purchase prices, usage estimates, maintenance costs and subscription amounts, is used to perform the calculations requested by you.

The calculator is designed to process these inputs directly in your browser. LifeCost does not require users to create an account to use its main calculation tools.

Browser Storage

LifeCost may use browser storage, such as local storage, to support website functionality or remember information locally. Information stored this way remains on your device unless the website's functionality specifically states otherwise.

You can manage or clear locally stored information through your browser settings.

Google Analytics

LifeCost uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website and to improve its content and functionality.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate usage patterns, browser type, device information and interactions with website content. This information is processed according to Google's applicable privacy policies and settings.

To learn more about how Google handles information, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

Cookies and Advertising

LifeCost may use cookies or similar technologies through third-party services such as analytics and advertising. These technologies may help measure website usage, remember preferences and support advertising services.

If Google AdSense or other Google advertising services are enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements.

Depending on applicable settings and regional requirements, users may be able to manage advertising preferences or personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings .

More information about how Google uses data from partner websites is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Third-Party Services

LifeCost may use third-party services for website analytics, advertising, hosting and other website-support functions. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

LifeCost does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

Data Security

LifeCost does not ask users to create an account to use its calculators. Calculation inputs are designed to be processed in the user's browser rather than submitted to a LifeCost user account.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information into the calculators.

Children's Privacy

LifeCost is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward children. The website does not knowingly request personal information from children through its calculator tools.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LifeCost's functionality, third-party services or applicable legal requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
