About LifeCost: A Smarter Way to Understand Purchase Costs

LifeCost is a free online collection of calculators and guides designed to help people understand the costs associated with buying, using and maintaining products and services.

When making a purchase, it is easy to focus only on the amount shown on the price tag. However, the initial purchase price does not always represent the full cost of owning or using something.

Maintenance, repairs, expected lifespan, usage frequency and resale value can all affect how much a purchase costs over time. LifeCost helps bring these factors together through simple calculations based on information entered by the user.

Why LifeCost was created

Two products can have very different purchase prices but provide different levels of value over their useful lifetimes.

For example, a laptop that costs ₹60,000 may last four years, while another that costs ₹45,000 may last three years. The cheaper option does not automatically have the lower cost per use.

To understand the difference, it can be useful to consider:

  • How much the product costs initially.
  • How long you expect to use it.
  • How frequently you plan to use it.
  • How much maintenance it may require.
  • Whether you expect to receive money from reselling it.

LifeCost brings these estimates together to help users look beyond the initial price and understand different aspects of ownership cost.

What LifeCost helps you calculate

Real ownership cost

Estimate the net cost of owning a product by combining its purchase price, expected maintenance expenses and estimated resale value.

Net cost = Purchase price + Total maintenance − Resale value

Cost per use

Estimate how much a product effectively costs each time you use it. This can help compare products with different prices, lifespans and usage patterns.

Cost per use = Net cost ÷ Estimated total uses

Product comparison

Compare two purchases using information such as purchase price, expected lifespan, weekly usage, maintenance and resale value. The comparison tool displays their estimated costs side by side.

Subscription expenses

Estimate how much recurring subscriptions may cost over a selected period. You can also consider how frequently you use a service to understand its estimated cost per use.

Other cost-planning tools

LifeCost also provides tools for exploring decisions such as buying versus renting and keeping versus replacing an existing item.

Each tool focuses on a different type of cost comparison and uses the information entered by the visitor.

Who can use LifeCost?

LifeCost is designed for people who want to understand the financial side of everyday purchase decisions.

  • Students: Estimate the ownership cost of laptops, phones and study-related purchases.
  • Everyday shoppers: Compare products before deciding which one better suits their needs.
  • Households: Estimate maintenance and long-term expenses for appliances and other household items.
  • Subscription users: Understand how recurring payments add up over months and years.
  • Budget-conscious buyers: Look beyond the initial price when planning a purchase.

How LifeCost works

LifeCost uses the values entered by visitors to calculate estimated costs. You provide the purchase details, expected lifespan, usage frequency, maintenance expenses and resale value where applicable.

The calculator processes these inputs and displays results such as:

  • Estimated net ownership cost.
  • Cost per use.
  • Cost per day.
  • Cost per month.
  • Average cost per year.
  • Estimated total uses.

You can change your inputs to explore different scenarios and understand how the results change.

Our approach to cost estimates

LifeCost aims to make cost calculations easier to understand by presenting the results in simple terms.

However, estimates depend on the accuracy of the information entered. Actual ownership expenses, product lifespan, usage and resale value may differ from expectations.

Not every possible expense is automatically included in every calculation. Taxes, delivery charges, financing costs, insurance and unexpected repairs may need to be considered separately.

LifeCost provides calculation tools and educational information. It does not guarantee future expenses, recommend specific products or replace independent research before making a purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Is LifeCost free to use?

Yes. LifeCost provides free online calculators and educational guides for estimating different types of purchase and ownership costs.

Does LifeCost recommend which product I should buy?

No. LifeCost calculates estimated costs using your inputs. You should also consider product quality, reliability, performance, warranty and personal requirements before making a decision.

Does LifeCost use actual market prices?

The calculators use the values entered by visitors. You should enter current prices and realistic estimates for the products or services you want to evaluate.

Are the calculator results guaranteed?

No. Results are estimates based on the information entered. Actual expenses and usage may change over time.

Can I use LifeCost for different types of purchases?

Yes. The main calculator can be used for different products, including electronics, clothing, shoes and household items, provided you can estimate their price, lifespan and usage.
